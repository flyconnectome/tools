Tools for neuroanatomy & connectomics

Libraries, apps and dataset packages to fetch, analyze, compare and visualize neurons and connectomes. Developed by the Drosophila Connectomics Group (University of Cambridge) and the Jefferis lab (MRC LMB), together with contributors from many other labs.

        Get in touch

        Questions, bug reports, ideas or want to contribute? We'd love to hear from you.

        Questions & bugs

        Open an issue on the tool's GitHub repository - every tool card above links to it. For open-ended questions, navis and Coda also have discussion boards.

        Tip: include a short code example, your package versions and the dataset you are using.

        Something missing here?

        Tool not listed or information out of date? Open an issue on flyconnectome/tools or edit tools.yaml directly.
